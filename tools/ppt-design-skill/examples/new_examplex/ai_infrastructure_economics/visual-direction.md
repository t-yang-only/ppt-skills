## Theme lock v1

- Palette: warm paper, ink, cobalt evidence, vermilion constraint, and muted green for operating leverage.
- Typography: oversized grotesk headlines; compact monospace metadata.
- Grid: 12-column field with persistent left rail and generous outer margin.
- Composition: one dominant action per page; asymmetric masses; rules and stepped geometry instead of repeated cards.
- Forbidden: three equal cards, generic dashboard grids, decorative arrows, tiny body copy, and empty space without a declared purpose.
